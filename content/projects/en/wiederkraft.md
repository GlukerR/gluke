## The task

WiederKraft supplies equipment for car service centres: lifts, tyre changers and related hardware. Photographing fully assembled machinery like this is labour-intensive, and the assets were needed in two qualities at once — for the website and for the printed catalogue.

The source data consisted of drawings and CAD files of the equipment.

## The work

We completed a series of projects for the digital catalogue:

- modeling of 20+ pieces of equipment from drawings and CAD files;
- catalogue renders on a light background and scenes in a service bay;
- schematic and blueprint-style presentations to explain how the equipment works;
- TIFF assets at 300 DPI for large-format printing.

## The result

A single set of assets covers both the web catalogue and print: the same model goes into the product card, the catalogue and large-format printing without re-modeling.
