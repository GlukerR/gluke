## The task

HouseGuru sells household products through marketplaces. The product pages needed more than catalogue imagery: they needed assets that explain how a product is built and how it is used.

The source data consisted of physical product samples.

## What GLUKE did

We produced more than 30 visuals:

- modeling of the products from a physical sample, keeping the proportions and details intact;
- catalogue renders and interior scenes for the product pages;
- exploded views breaking down the structure of the product;
- product animation;
- packaging artwork.

The animation and the packaging artwork were produced by Alexander Glukhov.

## The result

The client received a complete set of assets for its product pages: from product renders to exploded views and animation, plus the packaging artwork. All visuals share one style, so the products look consistent in the shared listing.

The work ran through 2024–2025 and is complete.
