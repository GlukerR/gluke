## The task

SibMaster produces automatic heating boilers and block-modular boiler houses. The client needed 3D models for the catalogue, technical cutaways of the internal structure and clear materials that explain to buyers how a boiler works.

The source data consisted of drawings, photographs and technical documentation.

## What GLUKE did

- four detailed 3D boiler models from drawings and photos;
- detailed modeling of the hardware: hopper with vibrator, sensors, control cabinet with cables;
- technical cutaways of the internal structure: grate, gas channels, water circuits;
- visualization of flame flows and the path of heat through the channels with arrows;
- combustion process animation: the path of the coal from the grate to the heat transfer to the water;
- catalogue renders in the formats and colours the client needed.

## The result

The client received a set of 3D assets for the catalogue and sales: models, cutaways and an animation that clearly explain to buyers how an automatic boiler works. The engagement ran under contract in 2024–2026 and is complete.
